# Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboards

A professional portfolio repository showcasing interactive data visualizations and analytical dashboards built using Power BI. 

---

## 📊 Dashboards Included

### 1. Virat Kohli Career Stats Analysis Dashboard
* **Overview:** An in-depth sports analytics dashboard tracking the career performance and batting metrics across multiple formats and years[cite: 8].
* **Key Metrics Tracked:** 
  * Total Innings (286), Career Runs (14K), Batting Average (57.36), and Strike Rate[cite: 8].
  * Annual run progression, boundary distribution (4s and 6s breakdown), and century/half-century trends over time[cite: 8].
* **Interactive Filters:** Global filters for Match Format (ODI, T20, Test) and individual calendar years (2008–2025)[cite: 8].
* **Preview:**
  > ![Kohli Career Analysis](screenshots/KohliCareerAnalysis.jpg)

### 2. Amazon Sales & Fulfillment Report
* **Overview:** A commercial supply chain and e-commerce sales performance dashboard monitoring financial metrics, logistics, and product category trends[cite: 9].
* **Key Metrics Tracked:** 
  * Total Revenue ($12M Sum of Amount)[cite: 9].
  * Fulfillment distribution split between Amazon and Merchant channels[cite: 9].
  * Courier delivery status (Shipped, Cancelled, Unshipped, etc.)[cite: 9].
  * Top 5 product categories (T-shirts, Shirts, Blazers, Trousers, Wallets) and geographical fulfillment across cities and states[cite: 9].
* **Interactive Filters:** Dynamic Date Range slider (e.g., April 2022 filtering)[cite: 9].
* **Preview:**
  > ![Amazon Report](screenshots/AmazonReport.png)

## 🛠️ Skills & Techniques Demonstrated
* **Data Modeling & Transformation:** Structuring multi-table datasets, establishing clean entity relationships, and managing data granularity.
